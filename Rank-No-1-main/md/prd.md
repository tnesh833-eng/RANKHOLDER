# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## What is Rank-Holder?
Welcome to the Rank-Holder project! This document explains what our product is and who it is built for. Rank-Holder is a premium career and artificial intelligence platform designed to help people grow professionally. Think of it as a smart online assistant that gives users the insights and tools they need to succeed in their careers.

## Who is this for?
Our main audience includes people who are focused on building their careers, technology professionals who want to use AI to work smarter, and students or entry-level job seekers looking for an edge in the job market.

## What can users do on the platform?
- **Create an Account:** Users can safely sign up, log in, and manage their personal accounts.
- **Use AI Tools:** We provide special features powered by artificial intelligence to help users enhance their career prospects.
- **Upgrade to Premium:** Users can pay a small subscription fee (starting at just a few rupees) to unlock premium features and exclusive content.
- **Admin Controls:** We have a special dashboard just for the platform owners to keep track of users and manage payments smoothly.

## How is it built?
To make this work, we use a technology called Node.js for the main engine of the website. We store user information safely in a lightweight database. When users want to pay for premium features, we use a trusted payment service called Razorpay to handle the money securely. Finally, the parts of the website that the user actually sees and clicks on are built using standard web design coding languages like HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
